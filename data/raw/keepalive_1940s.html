Bollywood Box Office

A unique compilation of Biggest Bollywood Blockbusters and Hit Movies since 1930s. You will find all top grossers of yesteryears in year wise and decade wise list with their performance and box office numbers. The list is also clickable to go to the song list section and for each hit movie their music album information is provided along with lyrics and relevant news about the movie. You can spend a good time knowing about some of the most loved Indian Hindi movies, their star cast and every song in that movie. Box Office is the general barometer for the Hindi Movies and keep Alive Box office section offers an in-depth and comprehensive figures for these super movies that have entertained and enthralled generations of movie loves across India and abroad. Which is the biggest hit in 1955? Top 10 movies of 1970s? You will get answers to all these questions and their directors, actors and all that is associated with these movies.
